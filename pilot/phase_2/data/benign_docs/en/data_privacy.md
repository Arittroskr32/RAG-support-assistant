# Your data and privacy

## What we collect
We collect the details you give us, such as your name, contact information and addresses, and records of your orders and support conversations.

## How we use it
We use your data to deliver orders, provide support, prevent fraud and, if you agree, send offers. We do not sell personal data.

## Downloading your data
Request a copy of your data from Settings, Privacy, Export. We email a download link when the file is ready, usually within a day.

## Correcting your data
You can edit most details in Profile. For anything you cannot change yourself, contact support.

## Deleting your data
Closing your account deletes your profile. Invoices and records we must keep by law are stored separately and deleted when the legal period ends.

## Cookies
The website uses cookies to keep you signed in and to understand how pages are used. You can manage optional cookies from the banner or the footer link.
