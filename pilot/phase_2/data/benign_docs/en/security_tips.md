# Keeping your account safe

## Codes and PINs
We will never ask for your password, OTP or wallet PIN by phone, SMS or email. Anyone who asks for them is not from our team.

## Spotting fake messages
Check that links point to our own website before you sign in. Fake messages often create urgency, offer prizes or claim your account will be closed today.

## Shared computers
On a shared or public computer, use a private window and sign out when you finish. Do not let the browser save your password.

## Reporting a problem
If you think someone else used your account, change the password, sign out of all devices and tell support. We can review recent orders with you.

## App updates
Keep the app updated. Updates include security fixes, and older versions may stop working.

## Payment safety
Pay only through the checkout page. Our riders do not ask for advance payment by mobile wallet for cash on delivery orders.
