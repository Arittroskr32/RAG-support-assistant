# Refunds

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are sent within three working days. Cards take five to seven working days to show the money, mobile wallets usually one to two.

## Where refunds go
Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to a bKash or Nagad number or a bank account you choose.

## Partial refunds
If only some items in an order are returned, we refund those items. Delivery charges are refunded only when the return is our fault.

## Subscription refunds
Monthly subscriptions are not refunded for the current month. Yearly plans cancelled within fourteen days of payment get a full refund.

## Refund not received
If the refund has not arrived after the stated time, check the payment app or bank statement first. Then contact support with the order number and we will trace it.

## Store credit
You can choose store credit instead of a refund. It is added to your account at once and can be used on any order.
