# Using the mobile app

## Installing the app
The app is available on Google Play and the App Store. Search for our name and check the publisher before installing.

## Supported phones
The app works on recent Android and iOS versions. On very old phones some features, such as camera upload, may not be available.

## Offline use
Your cart and recent orders are saved on the phone, but you need an internet connection to pay or track a parcel.

## App problems
If the app freezes, close it and open it again. If the problem continues, clear the app cache or reinstall it; your account and orders are not affected.

## Changing the language
Open Profile, Language and choose Bangla or English. The change applies at once.

## Dark mode
The app follows your phone's dark mode setting. You can also choose light or dark under Settings, Display.
