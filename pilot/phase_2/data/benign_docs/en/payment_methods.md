# Payment methods

## Accepted methods
We accept Visa and Mastercard, bKash, Nagad, Rocket and bank transfer. Cash on delivery is available for physical orders in most districts.

## Paying with bKash
Choose bKash at checkout and you are taken to the bKash payment page. Enter your account number, confirm with the OTP and your PIN on the bKash page only. Our staff never ask for your PIN.

## Saving a card
You can save a card for faster checkout. Card numbers are stored by our payment provider, not by us, and only the last four digits are shown in your account.

## Bank transfer
For bank transfer, use the account details printed on the invoice and add the invoice number as the reference. Transfers usually show up within two working days.

## Payment receipts
A receipt is emailed as soon as a payment is confirmed. You can also find it next to the order in My orders.

## Currency
All prices are in Bangladeshi taka. Cards issued abroad are charged in taka, and your bank converts the amount at its own rate.
