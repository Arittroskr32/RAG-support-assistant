# Signing in

## Forgotten password
On the sign-in page choose Forgot password and enter your email or mobile number. We send a reset link that works once and expires after a short time. Pick a new password you have not used before.

## Locked account
After several wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for fifteen minutes to protect it. Wait and try again, or reset the password to unlock it at once.

## Two-step verification
Turn on two-step verification under Settings, Security. Each sign-in on a new device then asks for a code from your authenticator app or an SMS. Keep the backup codes somewhere safe.

## New device alerts
When your account is used on a new phone or browser we send you an email. If it was not you, change your password and sign out of all sessions from the Security page.

## Signing out everywhere
Settings, Security, Active sessions lists every device that is signed in. You can end any single session, or choose Sign out of all devices if you lost a phone.

## Changing your email
To change the email on your account, open Profile and choose Edit next to the email. We confirm the change with a link sent to the new address and a notice sent to the old one.
