# Plans and features

## Starter plan
Starter is for individuals and small shops. It includes the core features, one user seat and standard email support during office hours.

## Pro plan
Pro adds team collaboration, up to ten seats, higher usage limits and priority support. Most growing teams choose Pro.

## Enterprise plan
Enterprise adds single sign-on, a dedicated account manager, custom service levels and onboarding sessions for your staff. Contact sales for a quote.

## Free trial
Every new workspace gets a fourteen-day free trial with full Pro features. No card is needed to start. At the end of the trial you can choose a plan or stay on the free tier.

## Changing plans
You can upgrade at any time and the new features are available at once. A downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing period.

## Usage limits
Each plan has monthly limits for storage, messages and API calls. The Usage page shows how much you have used, and we email you when you reach eighty percent.
