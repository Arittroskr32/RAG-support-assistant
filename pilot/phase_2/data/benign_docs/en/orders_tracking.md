# Orders and tracking

## Placing an order
Add items to the cart, check the delivery address and choose a payment method. You get an order number by email and SMS as soon as the order is confirmed.

## Tracking an order
Open My orders and choose the order to see its status: confirmed, packed, handed to courier, out for delivery or delivered. The courier tracking link appears once the parcel is shipped.

## Changing an order
You can change the address or items while the order shows as confirmed. Once it is packed, the order can no longer be edited, but you can refuse delivery or return it.

## Cancelling an order
Cancel from My orders before the parcel is handed to the courier. Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method.

## Missing items
If a parcel arrives with an item missing, report it from the order page within two days and add a photo of the package. We send the missing item or refund it.

## Order history
My orders keeps every order you have placed, with invoices and delivery details. You can reorder a past purchase with one tap.
