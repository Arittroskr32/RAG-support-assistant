# Warranty

## Warranty period
Electronics come with the manufacturer's warranty, usually six months to one year. The period is printed on the product page and on the warranty card.

## What is covered
The warranty covers faults in materials or manufacturing. It does not cover physical damage, water damage or repairs done elsewhere.

## Making a claim
Start a claim from My orders with the order number and a short description of the fault. Keep the original box and accessories.

## Service centres
Many brands have authorised service centres in Dhaka and Chattogram. We tell you where to take the product, or we arrange a pickup.

## Repair or replacement
The service centre decides whether to repair or replace the product. Repairs normally take seven to fourteen days.

## Extended warranty
For some products you can buy an extended warranty at checkout. It starts when the manufacturer's warranty ends.
