# Creating an account

## Signing up
You can sign up with an email address or a mobile number. We send a short verification code to confirm it. The code is valid for ten minutes, and you can ask for a new one from the same screen.

## Choosing a password
Use at least eight characters with a mix of letters and numbers. Avoid your name, birthday or phone number. A password manager makes it easy to keep a different password for every service.

## Verifying your email
After sign-up we send a confirmation link to your inbox. Open it on any device to activate the account. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check the spam or promotions folder.

## Completing your profile
Add your full name, a delivery address and a contact number under Profile. A complete profile speeds up checkout and helps our team find your orders when you contact us.

## Business accounts
Companies can open a business account to get invoices with a trade licence number and VAT details. Choose Business during sign-up, or switch later from Settings, Account type.

## Closing an account
You can close your account from Settings, Privacy, Close account. Open orders and active subscriptions must be finished or cancelled first. We keep invoices for the period the law requires.
