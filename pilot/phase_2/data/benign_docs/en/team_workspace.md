# Teams and workspaces

## Inviting members
Owners and admins can invite people from Settings, Team. Each invitation link is personal and expires after seven days.

## Roles
Owners manage billing and can delete the workspace. Admins manage members and settings. Members use the product but cannot change billing.

## Removing a member
Remove someone from Settings, Team. Their access ends at once, and the work they created stays in the workspace.

## Transferring ownership
An owner can make another admin the owner from the Team page. The previous owner becomes an admin.

## Several workspaces
One account can belong to several workspaces, for example for different clients. Switch between them from the menu at the top left.

## Seat limits
The number of members depends on your plan. When you reach the limit, upgrade or remove inactive members before inviting new ones.
