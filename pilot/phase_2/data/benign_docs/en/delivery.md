# Delivery

## Delivery times
Orders inside Dhaka usually arrive in one to two working days. Other districts take three to five working days, depending on the courier route.

## Delivery charges
Delivery is free above a minimum order value shown at checkout. Below it, a flat charge applies inside Dhaka and a slightly higher one elsewhere.

## Courier partners
We work with several courier companies. The one assigned to your order is shown on the tracking page together with the rider's contact once the parcel is out for delivery.

## Failed delivery
If the rider cannot reach you, they try again the next working day. After two failed attempts the parcel returns to our warehouse and we contact you.

## Delivery to an office
You can deliver to an office address. Add the company name and a floor or desk number so the rider can find you quickly.

## Damaged parcels
Check the package when it arrives. If it is visibly damaged, you can refuse it or take photos and report it from the order page the same day.
