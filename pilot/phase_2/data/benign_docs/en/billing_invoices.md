# Billing and invoices

## Billing cycle
Subscriptions are billed monthly or yearly, starting on the day you subscribed. A yearly plan costs less per month than paying monthly.

## Downloading invoices
All invoices are under Settings, Billing, Invoices. Each one can be downloaded as a PDF and includes your company details if you added them.

## Updating billing details
Change the billing name, address or VAT number on the Billing page. The new details appear on every invoice issued after the change.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails we try again after three days and email you each time. Update the payment method before the third attempt to avoid an interruption.

## Cancelling a subscription
Go to Settings, Billing, Cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and no further charges are made.

## Taxes
Prices on the website exclude VAT unless stated. The tax is calculated from your billing address and shown on the invoice.
