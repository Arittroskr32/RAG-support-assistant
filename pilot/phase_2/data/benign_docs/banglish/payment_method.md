# Payment method

## Ki ki method chole
Amra Visa ar Mastercard, bKash, Nagad, Rocket ar bank transfer nei. Beshirbhag district e product order e cash on delivery ache.

## bKash e payment
Checkout e bKash select korle bKash er payment page e niye jabe. Okhane account number din ar shudhu bKash er page ei OTP ar PIN diye confirm korun. Amader staff kokhono PIN chay na.

## Card save kora
Fast checkout er jonno card save korte paren. Card number amader payment provider rakhe, amra na, ar account e shudhu shesher char ta digit dekha jay.

## Bank transfer
Bank transfer er jonno invoice e chapano account details use korun ar reference e invoice number likhun. Shadharonoto dui working day er moddhe taka jama hoy.

## Payment receipt
Payment confirm hoyar shathe shathe receipt email e chole jay. My orders e order er pasheo receipt ta paben.

## Currency
Shob daam Bangladeshi takay. Bidesh theke issue kora card eo takay charge hoy, ar apnar bank nijer rate e convert kore.
