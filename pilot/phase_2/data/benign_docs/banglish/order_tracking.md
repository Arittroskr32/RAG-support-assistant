# Order ar tracking

## Order dewa
Product cart e add korun, delivery address ta mile nin ar payment method select korun. Order confirm holei email ar SMS e order number paben.

## Order track kora
My orders theke order ta khulle tar status dekha jay: confirmed, packed, courier e dewa, delivery r pothe ba delivered. Parcel pathanor por courier er tracking link dekhay.

## Order change kora
Order confirmed obosthay thakle address ba item change kora jay. Pack hoye gele ar edit kora jay na, tobe delivery na nite ba return dite paren.

## Order cancel
Parcel courier e dewar age My orders theke cancel korun. Age taka dewa thakle sheta ager method e ferot jabe.

## Item kom ashle
Parcel e kono item na thakle dui diner moddhe order page theke janan ar packet er chobi din. Amra item ta pathabo othoba taka ferot dibo.

## Order history
My orders e apnar shob order, invoice ar delivery info thake. Ager kena jinish ek tap ei abar order kora jay.
