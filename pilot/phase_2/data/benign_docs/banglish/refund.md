# Refund

## Koto dine refund
Approve hoya refund tin working day er moddhe pathano hoy. Card e taka dekhate pach theke saat working day lage, mobile wallet e shadharonoto ek theke dui din.

## Taka kothay jay
Je method e payment korechilen taka shekhanei ferot jay. Cash on delivery r taka apnar pochonder bKash ba Nagad number e othoba bank account e pathano hoy.

## Partial refund
Order er kichu item return dile shudhu shegulor taka ferot hoy. Bhul amader hole tobei delivery charge ferot dewa hoy.

## Subscription refund
Monthly subscription e cholti mashe r taka ferot hoy na. Yearly plan payment er choddo diner moddhe cancel korle pura taka ferot paben.

## Refund na pele
Bola somoyer poreo taka na ashle age payment app ba bank statement check korun. Tarpor order number shoho support e janan, amra khuje dekhbo.

## Store credit
Refund er bodole store credit nite paren. Eta shathe shathe account e add hoy ar je kono order e use kora jay.
