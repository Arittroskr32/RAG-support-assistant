# Delivery

## Delivery time
Dhakar bhitore shadharonoto ek theke dui working day e product pouchay. Onno district e courier route onujayi tin theke pach working day lage.

## Delivery charge
Checkout e dekhano minimum order value er beshi hole delivery free. Tar kom hole Dhakar bhitore ekta fixed charge ar baire ektu beshi charge lage.

## Courier company
Amra koekta courier company r shathe kaj kori. Apnar order er courier tracking page e dekha jay, ar parcel roana hole rider er contact number o thake.

## Delivery na hole
Rider apnake na pele porer working day te abar try koren. Duibar fail hole parcel warehouse e fere ashe ar amra apnar shathe jogajog kori.

## Office e delivery
Office address eo delivery newa jay. Company r naam ar floor ba desk number likhe din, jate rider shohoje khuje pan.

## Damaged parcel
Parcel hate peye packet ta dekhe nin. Sposto damage thakle na nite paren, othoba chobi tule shedin ei order page theke janan.
