# Support e jogajog

## Support time
Amader team shonibar theke brihoshpotibar shokal noyta theke raat aatta porjonto reply dey. Er baire pathano message er reply porer working day te dewa hoy.

## Live chat
Website er nicher kone ar app er Help e live chat ache. Support time e amader kache pouchanor eta shobcheye fast upay.

## Email support
Help page theke amader support address e likhte paren. Order number ar problem er choto description dile ek reply tei solve korte pari.

## Phone support
Pro ar Enterprise customer der jonno phone support ache. Login korar por Help page e number ta dekha jay.

## Ki ki hater kache rakhben
Order number, account er email ar error message er screenshot rakhun. Password ba OTP karo shathe share korben na, amader staff der shatheo na.

## Complaint
Reply te khushi na hole agent ke case ta escalate korte bolun. Ekjon team lead dui working day er moddhe sheta dekhen.
