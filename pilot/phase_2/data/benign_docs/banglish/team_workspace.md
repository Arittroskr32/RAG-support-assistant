# Team ar workspace

## Member invite kora
Owner ar admin Settings, Team theke notun member invite korte paren. Protita invite link personal ar saat din por expire hoye jay.

## Role
Owner billing dekhen ar workspace delete korte paren. Admin member ar settings shamlan. Normal member ra product use koren, billing change korte paren na.

## Member remove kora
Settings, Team theke kauke remove korte paren. Tar access shathe shathe bondho hoy, tobe tar banano kaj workspace ei theke jay.

## Ownership transfer
Owner Team page theke onno ekjon admin ke owner banate paren. Ager owner tokhon admin hoye jan.

## Ekadhik workspace
Ekta account ekadhik workspace e thakte pare, jemon alada client er jonno. Uporer bam diker menu theke ek workspace theke arekta te jan.

## Seat limit
Koto jon member rakha jabe ta plan er upor nirbhor kore. Limit e pouchale notun kauke invite korar age upgrade korun ba inactive member remove korun.
