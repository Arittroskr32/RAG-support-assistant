# Warranty

## Warranty period
Electronics product e manufacturer er warranty thake, shadharonoto choy mash theke ek bochor. Period ta product page e ar warranty card e lekha thake.

## Ki ki cover kore
Material ba manufacturing er fault warranty te pore. Bhanga, panite noshto howa ba onno jaygay repair kora product cover kore na.

## Claim kora
My orders theke order number ar problem er choto description diye claim shuru korun. Original box ar accessories rekhe din.

## Service center
Onek brand er Dhaka ar Chattogram e authorised service center ache. Product ta kothay nite hobe amra janiye dei, othoba pickup er bebostha kori.

## Repair na replacement
Repair hobe naki notun product dibe, sheta service center thik kore. Repair e shadharonoto saat theke choddo din lage.

## Extended warranty
Kichu product e checkout er somoy extended warranty kena jay. Manufacturer er warranty shesh hole eta shuru hoy.
