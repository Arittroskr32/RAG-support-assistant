# Plan ar features

## Starter plan
Starter plan individual ar choto dokan er jonno. Ete core feature gulo, ekjon user seat ar office time e normal email support thake.

## Pro plan
Pro plan e team collaboration, maximum dosh jon member, beshi usage limit ar priority support pawa jay. Barte thaka team gulo beshirbhag Pro e nay.

## Enterprise plan
Enterprise e single sign-on, nijosho account manager, custom service level ar staff der jonno onboarding session thake. Daam jante sales team er shathe jogajog korun.

## Free trial
Protita notun workspace choddo diner free trial pay, jekhane Pro er shob feature thake. Shuru korte card lage na. Trial shesh hole plan select korun ba free tier e thakun.

## Plan change kora
Je kono somoy upgrade kora jay ar notun feature shathe shathe chalu hoy. Downgrade porer billing period er shuru theke kaj kore.

## Usage limit
Protita plan e storage, message ar API call er monthly limit ache. Usage page e koto use hoyeche dekha jay, ar ashi percent e pouchale amra email kori.
