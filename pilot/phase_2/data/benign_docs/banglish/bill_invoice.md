# Bill ar invoice

## Billing cycle
Subscription er bill monthly ba yearly hoy, jedin subscribe korechen shedin theke count shuru. Yearly plan e mashe khoroch kom pore.

## Invoice download
Shob invoice Settings, Billing, Invoices e ache. Protitai PDF hishebe namano jay, ar company info dile sheta invoice e thake.

## Billing info update
Billing page theke naam, address ba VAT number change korun. Change er por theke protita notun invoice e notun info dekhabe.

## Payment fail hole
Payment fail korle amra tin din por abar try kori ar protibar email pathai. Service bondho howa erate tritiyo try er age payment method update kore nin.

## Subscription cancel
Settings, Billing, Cancel subscription theke cancel korun. Je period er taka diyechen tar shesh porjonto access thakbe, er por ar taka katbe na.

## Tax
Website er daame alada kore na bola thakle VAT dhora nai. Apnar billing address onujayi tax hishab kore invoice e dekhano hoy.
