# AuraOps Technologies — Product Catalog & Specifications

## 1. Product Portfolio Overview

AuraOps offers a modular suite of cloud-native deployment automation, observability, and pipeline security products. Customers can purchase products individually as standalone subscriptions, subscribe through usage-based metered billing, or license the unified platform suite.

---

## 2. Commercial Product Catalog & Pricing

### Product 1: AuraFlow CD (Continuous Delivery Engine)
* **Product SKU:** `AOP-PROD-FLOW`
* **Category:** CI/CD & Progressive Delivery Automation
* **Delivery Model:** SaaS or Self-Hosted Agent
* **Pricing:**
  * **Team Tier:** $29 / active developer seat / month
  * **Enterprise Tier:** $69 / active developer seat / month (includes 24/7 SLA & multi-region deployment targets)
  * **Annual Commitment Discount:** 15% discount on 12-month prepaid contracts
* **Key Features:**
  * Automated canary releases, blue-green deployments, and traffic shadowing
  * Multi-cluster Kubernetes & serverless (AWS ECS, Cloud Run) orchestration
  * Declarative GitOps pipeline synchronization
* **Add-ons:**
  * Dedicated Build Runner Cluster: $150 / runner / month

### Product 2: AuraGuard AI (Autonomous Canary Verification)
* **Product SKU:** `AOP-PROD-GUARD`
* **Category:** Automated Quality Gating & Instant Rollback
* **Delivery Model:** SaaS Control Plane
* **Pricing:**
  * **Starter Pack:** $199 / month (up to 10 microservices monitored)
  * **Scale Pack:** $599 / month (up to 50 microservices monitored)
  * **Overage / Metered Rate:** $12 / additional microservice / month
* **Key Features:**
  * Sub-second statistical anomaly detection on deployment telemetry (OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Datadog)
  * Zero-touch automatic rollbacks under 15 seconds upon SLI violation
  * Blast-radius segmentation by geolocation or HTTP headers

---

### Product 3: AuraSight Analytics (DORA & Engineering Intelligence)
* **Product SKU:** `AOP-PROD-SIGHT`
* **Category:** Observability & Executive Analytics
* **Delivery Model:** SaaS Dashboard
* **Pricing:**
  * **Flat Rate:** $450 / organization / month (unlimited viewers)
  * **Read-Only Stakeholder Seats:** Included free of charge
* **Key Features:**
  * Real-time automated DORA metrics tracking (Lead Time, Deployment Frequency, Change Failure Rate, MTTR)
  * Pre-deployment infrastructure cost-drift estimates
  * Cross-service dependency topology mapper

### Product 4: AuraShield Compliance (Policy as Code & Audit)
* **Product SKU:** `AOP-PROD-SHIELD`
* **Category:** Security & Governance
* **Delivery Model:** SaaS with Local Runner Enforcers
* **Pricing:**
  * **Tier:** $350 / repository cluster / month
* **Key Features:**
  * Open Policy Agent (OPA) policy enforcement prior to deployment
  * Cryptographic artifact signing and Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) verification
  * Tamper-evident 7-year audit log retention for SOC 2 and ISO 27001 readiness
